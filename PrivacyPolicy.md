Darkelf Labs Privacy Policy

Updated: October 2026

Darkelf Labs develops privacy-focused browsers and related open-source software.

Privacy is a core design principle of Darkelf projects.

Darkelf Browsers

Darkelf browsers are designed to minimize unnecessary collection, retention, and transmission of browsing information by Darkelf Labs.

Darkelf Labs does not operate an advertising network and does not intentionally build user profiles from browsing activity.

Darkelf browser projects may use local application data required for functionality, such as preferences, configuration, temporary session information, caches, or other browser state depending on the project and configuration.

Some Darkelf editions are specifically designed around ephemeral or non-persistent browsing.

Third-Party Websites and Services

Browsing the web necessarily communicates with websites and network services selected or requested by the user.

Darkelf may also integrate with or provide access to third-party technologies or services such as search engines, browser engines, filter-list providers, operating-system services, or other external resources.

Those third parties operate independently from Darkelf Labs and may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Darkelf Labs does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

Telemetry

Darkelf projects are designed to avoid unnecessary Darkelf-controlled telemetry.

Individual repositories and releases should document any functionality that communicates with a Darkelf-controlled or third-party service beyond ordinary user-requested browsing.

Local Data

Depending on the Darkelf project and configuration, application information may be stored locally on the user’s device.

Users remain responsible for the security of their devices, operating systems, accounts, backups, and locally stored information.

Security and Privacy Limitations

No browser can guarantee complete anonymity or prevent every form of tracking, fingerprinting, network observation, malware, exploitation, or information disclosure.

Darkelf privacy features reduce selected privacy risks but should not be interpreted as an absolute anonymity or security guarantee.

Open Source

Darkelf’s open-source projects can be independently inspected to evaluate their privacy behavior.

The source code and documentation contained in the applicable repository provide the most precise description of a particular project’s implementation.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the Darkelf ecosystem evolves.

Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this document.
